What is a Drone?

A drone simply refers to an unmanned vehicle, which includes land rover, submarines, and aircraft. However, the term is most commonly used to refer to unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) which is what this page is about.

Current Trends in Drone Technology

⦿ The drone industry is growing rapidly
⦿ UAVs will soon become much more common, spanning everyday uses like delivery and to military applications
⦿ AI integration is currently the biggest driver of innovation in the sector

⦿ A UK drone expo showcased that AI is being used to create increasingly powerful and fully autonomous drones.
⦿ As autonomy increases, cybersecurity become more critical to ensure that drone are not misused or hacked.
⦿ Stonger regulations will also be needed to control airspace management and avoid crashes between drones

Parts of a drone

Airframe / Body

⦿ Smaller drones are often made with injection molded plastic, though some can be 3D printed.
⦿ Higher performance drones tend to be made out of carbon fiber composite structures to withstand the greater forces and stresses of high speed flight.
⦿ The largest drones are often made out of aluminium alloys or titanium alloys to provide a strong and lightweight structure.

Propulsion

⦿ Electric brushless motors are the most common type of propulsion for drones which give efficiency and control.
⦿ Some drones use internal combustion engines for longer flight times and greater payload capacity.
⦿ Both types of propulsion use propellers to generate thrust. These can be made out of plastic, carbon fiber, or wood.
